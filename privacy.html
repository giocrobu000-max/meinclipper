Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who is responsible

Giovanni Crobu, Germany. Contact: giocrobu@gmail.com

Who uses this app

MeinClipper is used only by its owner to publish videos to his own TikTok account. There are no other users.

What data is processed

  • TikTok login data: when the owner connects his TikTok account, TikTok sends an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar). These are needed to publish videos.
  • Videos and captions: the videos, titles and hashtags the owner chooses to publish.
  • Publishing status: whether an upload succeeded, so the same video is never posted twice.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally and never shared. Data is sent only to TikTok, through the official TikTok API, to publish a video.

What we do not do

  • No data of other TikTok users is collected.
  • No data is sold, rented or given to third parties.
  • No advertising and no tracking cookies.

How long data is kept

Access tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app or they expire. The owner can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; local data is then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Write to the contact address above.
