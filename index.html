MeinClipper

A private publishing tool for one creator.

MeinClipper is a personal desktop tool used by a single creator (Giovanni Crobu) to prepare short videos and publish them to his own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

What it does

  • Cuts short vertical clips only from material the creator owns or has written permission to use (approved campaigns and creators).
  • Lets the creator review every video, title and hashtag before anything is published.
  • Publishes the approved video to the creator's own TikTok account and marks AI-generated content with TikTok's AI label.

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.
  • It never copies or uploads other people's clips.
  • It does not read, collect or sell data of other TikTok users.

Deutsch: MeinClipper ist ein privates Werkzeug für einen einzelnen Creator. Es veröffentlicht nur selbst geprüfte Videos über die offizielle TikTok-Schnittstelle auf dem eigenen Konto.
